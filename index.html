THE PROBLEM

Litter has a bigger journey than you think.

That wrapper dropped on the footpath can be blown or washed into a stormwater drain. From there, litter can reach rivers and eventually the ocean.

🛍️

01

Wildlife

Animals can mistake litter for food or become tangled in rubbish. Keeping waste out of habitats protects local ecosystems.

02

Waterways

Plastic and other rubbish can move through stormwater systems into creeks, rivers and the sea.

03

Our community

Litter makes public places less enjoyable and can cost councils money and time to clean up.

THE SOLUTION

Make the better choice easier.

You don't need to be perfect. Focus on practical habits that reduce the amount of waste you create and stop rubbish from becoming litter.

QUICK WIN

Keep a reusable bottle, small container and spare bag with you.
